About me

Based inBayelsa, Nigeria. Working with brands anywhere.

ToolsVS Code, GitHub, n8n, Telegram Bot API, AI video and image tools.

I help brands scale by making the boring parts run on their own.

I started with websites and stores, then saw the real problem for small businesses: the site looks good, but someone still has to take the order, check the payment and reply to every message. So I learned to build the system behind the site too.

Alongside that, I make animated clips for artists and storytellers, and UGC-style ads for product brands. Design, automation and animation together mean one person can launch your brand end to end.

I'm still growing, and I'm honest about it. Some projects below are practice or demo builds, and I label them so you know exactly what you're looking at.
